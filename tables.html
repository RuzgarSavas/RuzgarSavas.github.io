Coffee Shop Comparison

I compared three coffee shops in Ankara. Here's what I found.

Coffee Shop Prices and Ratings in Ankara
Coffee Shop Coffee (TL) Pastry (TL) Rating
Kafe Ankara 45 35 4.5
Bean There 55 40 4.8
Cup & Go 35 25 3.9
Prices are per item. Ratings are out of 5.

Three Conclusions

  1. Bean There is the most expensive but has the highest rating.
  2. Cup & Go is the cheapest option.
  3. Kafe Ankara is in the middle for both price and rating.
