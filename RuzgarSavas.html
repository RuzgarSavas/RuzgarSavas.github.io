Man Returns Lost Wallet, Gets Job Offer

← Back to News Hub

ANKARA – Someone dropped her wallet. She didn't notice.

She was walking on a steady street in Ankara. Lots of buildings. Parked cars. The walking pathway was narrow. Hard to pass without getting your bag stuck on something.

Her bag got stuck on a tree branch. When she got out her wallet fell. She didn't notice.

Mahmud saw that. He started to walk towards the wallet to give it back to her. She was in a hurry. He began to run to catch up to her. He catched up right in front of her office building.

He gave her the wallet back.

"Oh my God, thank you so much. I was in a hurry. I can't believe I didn't even notice it."

— The woman

Mahmud said, No problem. That's my duty to look up for others.

The woman liked how Mahmud behaved. She asked if he needed a job. She said she needed an assistant in her office.

Mahmud got a fresh job. He was a normal student that just graduated. He was struggling being a new graduate. Now he has a job.
