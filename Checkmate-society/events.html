Club Events

Practice, workshops, and tournament nights

Our schedule mixes relaxed games with structured learning and competitive events. Members can attend as often as their schedule allows.

Where we meet

Regular sessions are held at a fictional community activity room in central Istanbul. Special tournament locations are announced ahead of time.
