About the Club

A chess community built for steady improvement

Checkmate Society was founded in Istanbul in 2025 as a place where chess players can meet, learn from one another, and enjoy friendly competition.

What we value

  • Respect: Good sportsmanship matters before, during, and after every game.
  • Curiosity: Every game gives us something new to study.
  • Consistency: Regular practice is more useful than chasing quick results.
  • Community: Strong players help newer members grow.

Founded

2025

Location

Istanbul, Türkiye

Meetings

Tuesday and Friday evenings

Our goal

We want members to leave each meeting with one useful idea to try in their next game. That could be a new opening principle, a better way to calculate, or simply a lesson learned from a mistake.
