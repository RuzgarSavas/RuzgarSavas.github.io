Improve one habit at a time

Good chess improvement does not need to be complicated. Use a few repeatable habits and review the positions that teach you the most.

♟

Solve puzzles

Short tactical puzzles help you notice checks, captures, forks, pins, and other patterns faster.

♞

Review your games

After a game, find one turning point and ask what you could have done differently.

♜

Learn basic endgames

Start with king-and-pawn endings and common checkmates before studying rare positions.

♛

Play thoughtful games

Mix faster games with slower games that give you enough time to calculate and make a plan.

Trusted chess websites

Practice chess games and puzzles on Lichess.

Review piece movement and board setup with US Chess.

Read official chess news and information from FIDE.
