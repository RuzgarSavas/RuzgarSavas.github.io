Stephen Curry

Stephen Curry is a point guard known for his shooting and ball handling. He became the center of Golden State's offense and helped change how basketball teams use the three-point shot.

Kevin Durant

Kevin Durant joined Golden State as one of the NBA's top scorers. His height, shooting, and ability to score from almost anywhere made the Warriors even more difficult to defend.

Klay Thompson

Klay Thompson is known for his three-point shooting and defense. Along with Curry, he formed one of the most dangerous shooting combinations in basketball.

Draymond Green

Draymond Green gave the Warriors defense, passing, and leadership. His ability to guard different positions and create plays made him an important part of the team's system.

Andre Iguodala

Andre Iguodala was an experienced defender and versatile team player. He could defend important opponents, handle the ball, and contribute in many different areas of the game.
